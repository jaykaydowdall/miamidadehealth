– Affordable Rates With Full Coverage –

Group health insurance may be available for businesses with two or more employees who live or work in Miami-Dade, Broward, or Monroe Counties. Group plans can offer coverage options and flexibility to meet the health insurance needs of employers and employees.

Benefits and out-of-pocket costs vary by insurer and policy. Compare deductibles, copayments, networks, and covered services carefully before choosing a plan.

– Understanding Health Care Reform –

Health coverage requirements and options can be affected by federal and state rules, including the Affordable Care Act (ACA). Employers may wish to review current guidance on coverage, eligibility, and applicable requirements.

For up-to-date information, visit HealthCare.gov or speak with a qualified insurance professional.
