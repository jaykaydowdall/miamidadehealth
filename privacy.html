Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit Miami-Dade Health Insurance (this website) or call the telephone number shown here. This is an independently operated informational and lead-generation website, not the former Miami Dade Insurance.com business.

Depending on how you interact with the website or telephone service, information may include details you voluntarily provide, your phone number or caller identification, information you share during a call, and basic technical information associated with visits to the website. The website does not currently include a contact form. Collection by a telephone routing provider depends on the service configured.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, connect callers with relevant independent service providers, facilitate requested consultations or services, maintain or improve website and inquiry quality, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This website is designed to generate telephone inquiries about health insurance options. When you call, your inquiry may be routed or referred to an independent business or service provider that handles the services you are asking about. The website operator may be compensated for qualifying calls or referrals. Those providers operate independently, are responsible for their own services, and may follow their own privacy practices. Placing a call does not by itself mean you consent to unrelated marketing calls or messages.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Telephone inquiries may be routed or tracked through third-party telecommunications services. Depending on the configured service, call-related information such as the calling number, time, duration, and routing details may be processed. Call recording is not confirmed for this website. If recording is enabled, appropriate notice and consent must be provided where required by applicable law.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The current website files do not include a dedicated analytics script, advertising pixel, or website cookie feature. Standard hosting infrastructure may still process basic technical request information. If cookies or analytics are introduced later, this policy should be updated to describe their use.

7. Information Sharing

Information relevant to your inquiry may be shared with independent service providers who receive or respond to your call. Technical providers involved in website hosting, telephone routing, or security may also process information necessary to perform those functions. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled through the website and related services, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information should be retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here and applicable legal requirements. Specific retention periods depend on the systems and providers actually used.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to ask about personal information held about you, request correction, or request deletion. The website operator must provide an appropriate contact method for such requests before the site is published. Requests involving an independent service provider may need to be directed to that provider.

Links on this website and referrals made by telephone may lead to independent third-party websites or businesses. Their information handling and services are governed by their own policies and practices, not this Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the website, telephone arrangements, or information practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Before publishing: Confirm the actual website operator identity and provide a working privacy-contact method.
